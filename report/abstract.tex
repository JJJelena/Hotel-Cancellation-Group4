\begin{abstract}
We study hotel booking cancellation through a common workflow for data preparation, exploratory analysis, booking segmentation, classification, and model interpretation. The cleaned dataset contains 118,564 bookings from two Portuguese hotels, of which 37.26\% were cancelled. Using the same 24 inputs and fixed training folds, random-holdout average precision is 0.373 for the constant-prior baseline, 0.801 for logistic regression, 0.911 for random forest, and 0.883 for XGBoost. The forest's F1 is 0.795 at threshold 0.50, compared with 0.803 at the training-selected threshold 0.45; it falls to 0.580 on unseen input profiles and to 0.539 in a separately tuned chronological check. Five descriptive segments show substantial variation in cancellation rates, while forest explanations highlight country, agent, lead time, and previous cancellations. The results support a retrospective decision-support benchmark, subject to field availability, repeated profiles, temporal transfer, calibration, and intervention costs.
\end{abstract}
