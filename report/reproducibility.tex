\section{Reproducibility Checks}
The analytical record separates reference experiments from integration checks. Data cleaning was rerun from the raw CSV, reproducing all 32 cleaned columns with zero differences. Random train/test identifiers were reproduced in their supplied order, and all 94,851 fixed validation-fold assignments matched. The selected five-cluster fit was rerun, reproducing its original cluster sizes; the alternative-\(k\) and seed diagnostics remain the executed notebook record.

For Dummy, logistic regression, its no-agent variant, random forest, and its no-agent variant, all saved prediction rows were checked against the common data and split identifiers. Recomputed metrics matched to at most \(1.4\times10^{-16}\). Re-prediction with the saved random-training estimators differed from six-decimal exports by at most \(5.01\times10^{-7}\), consistent with rounding. The saved chronological forests also matched full-precision exports, and all five independent chronological validation windows preceded the test period. The SHAP reconstruction check covers all 400 explained rows.

The completed XGBoost study fitted all three configurations within the fixed five folds, then fitted its selected random and earlier-arrival models. Independent checks reproduced its metrics, configuration selection, 114 raw/sigmoid validation-grid rows across three models, and 12 test-assessment rows. The sigmoid maps were refitted from the training OOF exports and their test transformations checked. These checks do not imply that all historical model hyperparameter searches were fully retrained during integration or that the OOF protocol is nested.

Source-built preprocessing reproduces the original template's 221 encoded feature names and all random-test transformation values exactly. This avoids requiring the historical template pickle for new fitting. The integration environment used Python 3.11.2 and scikit-learn 1.9.1; the historical forest environment records scikit-learn 1.9.0, while the logistic regression metadata record 1.9.1. Original environment metadata are retained. Compatibility was verified for the recorded loading environment rather than assumed for arbitrary package versions.

The principal executable checks are \texttt{src/verification.py} and \texttt{src/verify\_e.py}. New training through \texttt{src/run\_project.py} writes to a separate run directory. The standard prediction key is model, variant, experiment, subset, and row identifier; validation records additionally carry the fixed fold number. Full-precision E probabilities and the six-decimal historical exports are both evaluated at their documented precision. This preserves an auditable relationship between the report, saved predictions, and code.
